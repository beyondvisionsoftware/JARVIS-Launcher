1. On-Device Local Data & Permissions

The following features access device resources strictly to deliver launcher and system HUD functionality. All data is processed in-memory or stored within your device's isolated application sandbox:

  • Installed Applications (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Scans installed applications to populate your home grid, app drawer, search bar, dock, folders, recent apps carousel, and hidden apps vault. Cached solely on-device.
  • Contacts & Calling (READ_CONTACTS, CALL_PHONE): Enables rapid contact searching and hands-free voice commands (e.g., "Call Mom" or direct call shortcuts). Contact queries happen strictly in volatile memory and are never collected, logged, or uploaded.
  • Calendar & Schedule (READ_CALENDAR): Powers the home calendar widget, schedule views, and event sheets. Calendar data is read in real-time on-device and never transmitted off-device.
  • Music & Local Audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Indexes locally stored audio files for the built-in JARVIS music player, Quick Settings tile, and HUD music widget using Android Media3 / ExoPlayer.
  • Images & Wallpapers (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Allows the Liquid Glass rendering engine to sample your active wallpaper backdrop for optical refraction, dynamic blur shaders, and album art display. Personal photos, camera rolls, and documents are never read, cataloged, or uploaded.
  • Real-Time Hardware & Diagnostics (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): Queries battery level, charging status, battery temperature, RAM utilization, storage stats, and network transfer speeds (upload/download) to render dynamic HUD status widgets and the Power House interface. This performance data is volatile/in-memory and never sent off-device.
  • Bluetooth Audio Routing (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_ADMIN): Detects connected audio output routes (Bluetooth headphones/speakers) to compensate for wireless latency within the audio-visualizer engine. No background beacon tracking or location scanning is performed.
  • Microphone & Voice Input (RECORD_AUDIO): Powers voice commands and speech input for the AI assistant via Android's native SpeechRecognizer. Raw microphone audio recordings are never saved to persistent storage or uploaded to our servers.
  • Alarms & Notifications (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Manages timers, exact HUD alarms, and media playback notification controls directly on-device.
  • System Settings & Widgets (WRITE_SETTINGS, MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS, BIND_APPWIDGET, REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES): Allows toggling brightness/volume controls, pinning standard Android widgets to the home screen, and initiating app uninstallation upon your request.
  • Accessibility Service (JarvisAccessibilityService): An optional service strictly used for system convenience gesture: double-tap to lock screen (GLOBAL_ACTION_LOCK_SCREEN). Configured with canRetrieveWindowContent="false"—it cannot inspect window content, read keystrokes, or monitor user interactions.

2. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

The launcher connects to network endpoints only when you explicitly use online-dependent features:

  • Weather Forecasts (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Your approximate coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent over secure HTTPS via our Cloudflare Worker proxy to WeatherAPI.com and MET Norway (api.met.no) to fetch local weather, AQI, and 5-day forecasts. Requests are anonymous—no user accounts, device serials, or advertising IDs are attached.
  • Generative AI Chat Assistant (Gemini / Groq / OpenRouter): When chatting with the AI assistant, your submitted prompt text is transmitted directly and securely (TLS/HTTPS) to your selected AI provider (Google Gemini, Groq, or OpenRouter) to generate a response. Beyond Vision Software does not operate any intermediary prompt logging servers. Data sent to third-party AI providers is governed by their respective terms:
    • • Google Privacy Policy
    • • Groq Privacy Policy
    • • OpenRouter Privacy Policy
    Your custom API keys and chat histories are stored strictly locally in private on-device storage.
  • External Web Searches: Web or video searches (e.g., YouTube or Google) are launched via standard Android system intents directly in your chosen browser or native app.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDK Disclosures

A. Google AdMob

JARVIS Launcher integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (com.google.android.gms.ads) solely to offer optional Rewarded Video Ads:

  • Purpose: Allows users to voluntarily watch a video ad to recharge in-app JARVIS gems or unlock HUD presets for free without purchasing a paid subscription.
  • User Initiated: Rewarded ads are never displayed automatically; they only load and run when you explicitly tap to watch an ad.
  • Data Collected by Google: Google AdMob may process standard device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), coarse location, IP address, and ad diagnostics in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Ad Controls: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android System Settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

B. Zero Behavioral Tracking or Telemetry

JARVIS Launcher contains no third-party user tracking SDKs, analytics suites, or telemetry tools (e.g., Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, or Mixpanel). We do not build user profiles or track your cross-app activity.

4. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

All launcher configuration data, widget layouts, custom styles, gem balances, and AI conversation histories reside locally on your device.

You retain full sovereignty over your data. You can permanently erase all stored application data at any time by clearing application data in Android System Settings:
Settings > Apps > JARVIS Launcher > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

JARVIS Launcher is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under 13.

6. International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

We respect user privacy rights globally, including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA frameworks:

  • Right to Access & Portability: All preferences and chat records are stored locally on your device in standard Android storage formats.
  • Right to Erasure: Clearing app data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell or monetize personal data. Ad personalization can be turned off in device settings.
